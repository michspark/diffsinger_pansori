## Retrieval and quality notes

- No requested paper or repository note was unobtainable. All paper entries have a non-trivial text extraction containing the abstract/opening and body/reference material available in the source PDF.
- `liu2023flow` uses the verified arXiv v1 PDF because the OpenReview PDF endpoint returned HTTP 403 to the downloader; the front matter records that choice.
- `openvpivocoders` has no `README.md` in its repository; its rendered landing page is sourced from raw `index.md`, which is recorded in that note.
- Papers with equation/figure replacement glyphs are explicitly flagged at the top of their Markdown files. These are text-extraction limitations, not missing PDFs.
- `ju2022telemelody` uses OCR because its embedded font encoding defeats standard extraction; its Markdown begins with an OCR extraction note.

Replacement-glyph flags: `liu2022diffsinger` (42), `liu2023flow` (32), `li2024rosvot` (3), `sheng2021songmass` (3), `chung2025kad` (4), `wu2023clap` (1), `li2024mert` (2), `kim2018korean` (65), `zhang2023visinger2` (4), `he2023rmssinger` (6), `zhang2024stylesinger` (1).
